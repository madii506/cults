the fine print

questions.

does cults hold my coins?

no. a vow is a memo transaction you sign. your coins never move and nothing is locked. the vow is a promise, and the chain is the witness.

what breaks a vow?

holding less than you swore at any moment before the vow ends: selling, sending to another wallet, or closing the account. it is read from your token account's own history. swearing more than you hold counts too.

what happens when it ends?

the vow is kept forever and its faith stays with you. you are free to sell. swear a new one to keep climbing.

who can bless?

anyone. most often the dev, from creator fees. the split is by faith, the transfers are public, and every batch carries a memo so the blessing shows on the cult's page.

what does it cost?

a vow costs the solana network fee, a fraction of a cent. cults takes nothing from vows or blessings.

is there a database?

no. every vow and blessing touches one public address, so the whole site is rebuilt from the chain on every read. anyone can check it.

is this financial advice?

no. memecoins can go to zero. a vow is a promise to yourself and your cult, not an investment.
